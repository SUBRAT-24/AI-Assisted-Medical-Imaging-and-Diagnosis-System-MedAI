Step 2

All raw DICOM studies undergo HIPAA de-identification (`pydicom`), Hounsfield Unit rescaling, isotropic voxel spacing resampling (`Spacingd(pixdim=(1.0, 1.0, 1.5))`), intensity clipping (`[-1000, 400] HU`), and stochastic 3D augmentations tracked via **DVC**. 

stages:
  preprocess_dicom:
    cmd: python backend/preprocess.py --config params.yaml --output data/processed
    deps: [backend/preprocess.py, backend/dicom_utils.py]
    params: [preprocess.xray_image_size, preprocess.ct_mri_patch_size]

Step 7 • Safety, Fairness & Limitations

Documented Model Limits, Bias & Regulatory Notice

NOTICE: For education and research only — never for clinical diagnostic use.
1. Shortcut Learning on ICU Hardware: Chest X-ray classifiers can attend to chest tubes, ECG wires, or portable AP markers as spurious proxies for Pneumothorax or Effusion. Mandatory Grad-CAM review enables radiologists to reject confounder-driven predictions.
2. Acquisition & Demographic Shift: Training cohorts (NIH Clinical Center, Stanford CheXpert, BraTS) underrepresent pediatric patients and vary across scanner kVp and reconstruction kernels.
3. NLP Report-Label Uncertainty: Automated report-mined labels carry ~5–8% noise on borderline findings (Infiltration vs. Consolidation).
